## Executive finding

The 20-question legacy batch contains only a small number of questions that remain within the declared scope of ST2. Most questions import divisibility, primes, intervals, polynomial equations, calculus, matrices, geometry, decimal expansions, or general set operations. These are separate mathematical ideas and should not be used to inflate the Inclusion Relations among Number Sets catalogue.

The batch is therefore **quarantined as legacy material**. Correct answers do not make an out-of-scope question suitable for this subtopic.

## Reuse policy

1. Preserve the original CSV and its question IDs as historical material.
2. Q001 and Q003 may be used as simple foundation references, but are not automatically approved as final question instances.
3. Q009, Q010 and Q018 require a deliberate scope decision before any reuse.
4. Do not copy routed questions into ST2 under superficial wording changes.
5. Any replacement must test inclusion among the declared number sets, or a directly justified inference about those sets, without requiring an unrelated chapter concept.

## Quality-control consequence

The existence of 20 legacy rows does not mean ST2 has 20 approved questions. The approved production count remains **zero** until a scope-compliant archetype set and a newly validated question batch are completed.
