QUESTION POSSIBILITY DEFINITION

A Question Possibility (QP) is an
academically distinct examinable possibility.

A QP represents:

WHAT can be asked.

A QP is NOT:

• A question
• An MCQ
• An archetype
• A solving method
• A trick
• A shortcut
