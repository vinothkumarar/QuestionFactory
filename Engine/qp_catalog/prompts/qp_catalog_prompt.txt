DEFINITIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

Concept:
A specific academic idea or knowledge component.

Question Possibility:
A distinct examinable possibility that can be asked
from a concept.

Archetype:
A recurring pattern used to construct questions
for a Question Possibility.

------------------------------------------------------------
DISCOVERY RULES

1. Discover ALL concepts.
