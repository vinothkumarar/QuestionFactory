**Authority:** National Testing Agency (NTA)  
**Official documents page:** https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/documents/  
**Official syllabus PDF:** https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3f8e59f4b2fe7c5705bf878bbd494ccdf/uploads/2026/10/20261024683680768.pdf

Audit findings:
- The NTA documents page lists the 2027 syllabus document.
- The PDF was opened and its subject sections were inspected.
- It contains Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry syllabus units.
- The PDF is the primary authority for Main 2027 mapping; coaching summaries and historical syllabi are not substitutes.

Subject structure used for the initial crosswalk:
- Mathematics: 14 units.
- Physics: 20 units, including Unit 20 Experimental Skills.
- Chemistry: 20 units, including Unit 13 Purification and Characterisation and Unit 20 Principles Related to Practical Chemistry.

- The 2026 syllabus PDF is therefore the latest published official syllabus available for this P1 audit.

- Do not label the 2026 syllabus as the official Advanced 2027 syllabus.
- Re-run the crosswalk when the official 2027 syllabus is published; compare every change before approving production scope.

## 3. Source integrity and use

- Primary official source documents only were used for the official-syllabus mapping.
- The NTA 2027 PDF and JEE Advanced 2026 PDF were opened and inspected directly.
- Page/line evidence is recorded in `P1_TAXONOMY_MAPPING_REGISTER.csv` and the subject scope reconciliation documents.
- No syllabus text was inferred from coaching websites where official text was available.
- Where an internal subtopic is not explicitly named, it is marked partial or no-direct-scope rather than automatically treated as included.

## 4. Required revalidation trigger

When the official JEE Advanced 2027 syllabus becomes available:
1. Preserve the 2026 source snapshot.
2. Download and hash the new official PDF.
3. Compare subject, unit and topic text against the 2026 baseline.
4. Update the mapping register with explicit added/removed/changed scope.
5. Revalidate all Main/Advanced-only and partial topics.
6. Do not generate or release production questions from changed scope until the new mapping passes review.
