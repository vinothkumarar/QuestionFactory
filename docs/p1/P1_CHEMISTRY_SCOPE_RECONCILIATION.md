## Main 2027 coverage gaps and taxonomy misclassification

### 1. Purification and Characterisation of Organic Compounds — Main Unit 13

Main Unit 13 explicitly includes:
- Crystallisation, sublimation, distillation, differential extraction and chromatography.
- Qualitative detection of N, S, P and halogens.
- Quantitative estimation of C, H, N, halogens, S and P.
- Empirical/molecular formula calculations and organic quantitative-analysis numericals.

Current internal evidence:
- `C20_CH1_ST9 Chromatographic Methods` exists under **C20 Metallurgy**, not under an organic purification chapter.
- No dedicated internal purification/characterisation unit or chapter is evident.
- C21 General Organic Chemistry covers classification, nomenclature, isomerism, electronic effects, intermediates, acidity/basicity, aromaticity and mechanisms; it does not provide the full Main Unit 13 purification/quantitative-analysis scope.

Disposition: partial mapping exists for chromatography only; the remaining official Unit 13 coverage is a taxonomy gap pending full subtopic search. The mapping register records C20_CH1_ST9 as a Main Unit 13 cross-unit candidate, not as Metallurgy.

### 2. Principles Related to Practical Chemistry — Main Unit 20

Main Unit 20 includes functional-group/element detection, specified inorganic/organic preparations, titrimetric exercises, qualitative salt analysis and practical experiments.

No dedicated internal Practical Chemistry unit/chapter is evident from the current 29-unit list. Do not infer coverage from Metallurgy, General Organic Chemistry or the existence of a Chromatographic Methods subtopic.

Disposition: explicit coverage gap pending an experiment/skill-level mapping.

### 3. Vitamins — Main Unit 19

Main Unit 19 Biomolecules explicitly includes classification and functions of vitamins. Internal `C27_CH3 Vitamins` exists but contains **zero subtopic rows** and is disabled.

JEE Advanced 2026 Biomolecules covers carbohydrates, proteins and nucleic acids in the retrieved syllabus text but does not explicitly include vitamins. Therefore this is a Main 2027 coverage requirement, not an assumed Advanced requirement.

### 4. Scope split across Main and Advanced

The following internal units have clear Advanced 2026 scope but are not standalone Main 2027 units:
- C4 States of Matter (some individual topics also map into Main U7/U9).
- C12 Surface Chemistry (some catalytic content may overlap Main U8; adsorption/colloids/micelles are not explicit).
- C14 Hydrogen.
- C15 detailed s-Block chemistry (Main U9 covers classification/trends, not the full chapter).
- C20 Metallurgy.
- C28 Polymers.
- C29 Chemistry in Everyday Life.

Do not disable or delete these internal records automatically. Keep them Advanced-only or partial until the exact subtopic crosswalk is approved.
