## Key conclusion

Internal Mathematics unit numbers and titles do not correspond one-to-one with official JEE Main/Advanced units. The internal U1 Number System is not an official standalone JEE syllabus unit. It is a mixed catalogue containing foundational number-theory topics plus a small number of functions topics that do map to official syllabi.

The internal U2 Algebra unit is also a mixed container: its chapters map across official Main Units 1–6 and 13. U9 Functions and U15 Sets and Relations overlap substantially with U2 and with each other. The taxonomy must therefore be reconciled at chapter/subtopic level, not by matching unit titles.

### U1 impact

**U1_CH1_ST1 — Natural Numbers, Whole Numbers and Integers currently has no direct official JEE Main 2027 or JEE Advanced 2026 topic mapping.** It must not be selected as the JEE pilot or receive JEE Main/Advanced production questions merely because it is the first existing catalogue. It may remain useful as foundational school-level content or for a separately authorised non-JEE track, but that is a different scope decision.

The same caution applies to most of U1: number classification, divisibility, HCF/LCM, Euclidean algorithm, modular arithmetic, cyclicity, remainders and base conversion are not explicit official syllabus topics in the two current authorities reviewed. No IDs have been removed or changed.

U2 is not equivalent to a single official “Algebra” unit. Its chapters must retain separate official mappings.

## U9 — Functions

- U9_CH1 Sets and Relations maps to Main Unit 1 and Advanced Sets/Relations/Functions, but duplicates U2_CH1 and U15_CH1.
- U9_CH2 Concept of Functions maps to Main Units 1/7 and Advanced Sets/Relations/Functions.
- U9_CH3 Domain, Codomain and Range maps more directly to Advanced, which explicitly names domain, codomain and range; Main function scope is broader and less explicit.
- U9_CH4 Types of Functions maps polynomial, rational, logarithmic and exponential functions to Main Unit 7. Modulus and greatest-integer functions are explicit in Advanced 2026 but not in Main 2027's enumerated function list.
- U9_CH5 Composition of Functions maps composition; associative/repeated-composition and functional-equation extensions require separate scope review.
- U9_CH6 Inverse Functions maps inverse functions; inverse-trigonometric connections map to Main Unit 14 and Advanced Trigonometry.
- U9_CH7 Graphs and Transformations maps simple function graphs to Main Unit 7. Detailed translation, scaling, reflection, modulus transformations and broad advanced graph problems require restrictions.

## U15 — Sets and Relations

- U15_CH1 Sets and Operations on Sets maps to Main Unit 1 and Advanced Sets/Relations/Functions. Venn diagrams are a representation technique, not explicitly named; cardinality and inclusion-exclusion need separate review.
- U15_CH2 Intervals and Number Line is only a partial match to sets/representations; intervals and number-line procedures are not explicitly enumerated.
- U15_CH3 Relations maps relation types and equivalence relations. Cartesian product is explicit in Advanced 2026 but not separately named in Main 2027; equivalence classes are not separately named.
- U15_CH4 Functions and Mapping maps functions, one-one, into, onto and composition. Domain/codomain/range and mapping diagrams are clearer in Advanced.
- U15_CH5 Types of Functions: polynomial and logarithmic/exponential functions map to Main Unit 7; absolute value and greatest-integer functions are explicit in Advanced 2026 but not Main 2027; periodicity maps more directly to Advanced Trigonometry.
- U15_CH6 Composition and Inverse Functions maps composition/inverse functions. Functional equations and applications are not an explicit standalone topic in the reviewed official sources.

## Duplicate and overlap impact

The exact-name audit found multiple repeated set/function subtopics across U2, U9 and U15:
- Basic Set Concepts, Representation of Sets, Operations on Sets, Venn Diagrams.
- Definition of Functions, One-One and Onto, Many-One and Into.
- Polynomial, Modulus, Greatest Integer, Exponential/Logarithmic and Periodic Functions.
- Inverse Functions and Existence of Inverse Functions.
- Equivalence Relations.

The same learning outcome must not be manufactured three times simply because three taxonomy IDs exist. However, stable IDs must remain preserved until a semantic comparison, lineage plan and explicit migration decision are approved.

## Candidate pilot exclusion and shortlist

- Exclude U1_CH1_ST1 from the JEE pilot shortlist unless new authoritative syllabus evidence is found.
- Prefer subtopics with explicit official scope and matched primary-source PYQs. The current strongest candidates are Physics P1_CH1_ST6 Errors and Measurements and P1_CH1_ST4 Dimensional Analysis, documented in `P1_CANDIDATE_PILOT_SEQUENCE.md`.
